3D playable ad · three.js

Mech Arena

Tap enemy mechs, charge a missile strike, finish on an end card with a store CTA.

  • One self-contained HTML per ad network — Meta, Google, MRAID — 560 KB, checked against a budget on every build
  • Zero allocations in the frame loop: slot pools and InstancedMesh, 60 FPS at ≤ 46 draw calls
  • No assets: procedural mechs, canvas textures, WebAudio sound

Merge Guard

  • Board, merge rules, path and game flow are pure modules with tests; scripted first summons for onboarding

Tool · three.js · computational geometry

Region Selector 3D

Trace regions on a floor plan or a 360° panorama: rooms become 3D walls and floors, zones become hotspots on a sphere.

  • Own geometry: ear-clipping triangulation, self-intersection checks, mitered polygon offset
  • Panorama picking without ray–sphere intersection: camera ray → longitude/latitude → pixel → zone
  • GLB export for Blender, undo/redo, 34 tests
